    Cleanup cannot be undone. Back up Emby and wait for a successful backup before removing any entries.
    
                
    Cleanup removes generated folders and station entries from the Emby TuneIn channel; it does not remove the channel itself, settings, or media files. Playlists and other references may break.
    
                
    For one category, compatible saved snapshots of other categories are retained. Refresh can recreate an enabled category; rebuilding it requires complete discovery and may reconcile other categories from their saved snapshots.
